HOW IT WORKS

A market, not a mystery.

Each dish has a base price. The market price adjusts according to the number of units sold in the previous 30 minutes.

  • Low demand can reduce the price by 2%.
  • Higher demand can increase it by up to 8%.
  • Market price is capped at 110% of base price.
  • Your checkout price is locked into the order.

This is a demonstration of a pricing concept, not a live financial market.
