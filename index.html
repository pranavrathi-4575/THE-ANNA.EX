THE ANNA.EX / OUR STORY

India's Flavour Exchange.

More than a menu. It's a flavour exchange.

THE ANNA.EX is a concept built around bringing the excitement of India's food culture together with the dynamics of a stock exchange.

We imagine a food marketplace where every dish has its own identity, demand and market movement. Inspired by financial exchanges, THE ANNA.EX explores a new way to experience food discovery and ordering.

Our goal is to combine technology, Indian flavours and an engaging marketplace experience.

Discover. Decide. Dine.

Currently a restaurant-ordering prototype based in Bengaluru, Karnataka.
